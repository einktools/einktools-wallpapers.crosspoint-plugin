einktools Wallpapers

Public-domain art from einktools.com/wallpapers, already sized for your screen and dithered to the 4 grays the Xteink panel shows.

1. Open this plugin, pick your model (X4 and X4 Pro, or X3) and a picture.
2. It is saved to /.sleep on the SD card.
3. Settings > Sleep Screen > Custom. The reader picks a random picture from /.sleep each time it sleeps.

If /sleep.bmp exists it is shown instead of the /.sleep pictures. Delete or rename it to use the rotation.

Make your own from any photo at einktools.com/xteink-wallpaper-converter
